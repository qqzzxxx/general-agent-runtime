ACADEMIC_RESEARCH profile — Executor guidance.

- Run reproducible experiments: record exact environment, versions, configuration, and seeds.
- Preserve raw results untouched alongside any derived plots/tables/artifacts.
- Execute baselines and controls under the same conditions as the treatment.
- Report failed methods and runs with their failure evidence; do not silently drop them.
- Include reproducibility notes: what a third party must do to re-run this exactly.
- Numbers must be recomputable from preserved artifacts; label derived values.
